## System Priority

The uploaded reference image is provided solely as a visual reference for creating a fictional avatar.

The objective is **NOT** to recreate the real person.

Instead, create a **new fictional individual** that preserves the overall visual essence of the reference while having a **different facial identity**.

---

## What to Preserve

The avatar should retain the recognizable style of the reference, including:

- Hairstyle
- Hair color
- Outfit
- Accessories
- Body proportions
- Age range
- Skin tone
- Posture
- Body language
- Expression style
- Photographic style
- Environment
- Lighting

---

## Facial Identity Redesign

The face **must belong to a different person.**

Redesign the facial identity by subtly modifying the biometric characteristics while maintaining the same overall aesthetic.

Change naturally:

- Eye shape and proportions
- Eyebrow shape and thickness
- Nose shape and bridge
- Nostril structure
- Lip shape and volume
- Smile geometry
- Cheekbone definition
- Jawline
- Chin shape
- Ear shape
- Facial proportions
- Facial asymmetry
- Forehead proportions
- Skin micro-features
- Facial contours

---

## Key Constraints

The facial modifications should be subtle enough that the avatar still conveys the same overall vibe and age, yet significant enough that it is clearly **another individual**, not the original person.

Think of the result as a **fictional character inspired by the reference** rather than a portrait of the reference.

The hairstyle, clothing, accessories, pose, lighting, composition, environment, and photographic style should remain highly consistent with the reference, while the face becomes a new, original identity.

---

## Expected Output

The final image should look like a real photograph of a different person wearing the same hairstyle, clothing, and accessories as the reference — not a clone, not a lookalike, and not a biometric duplicate.
